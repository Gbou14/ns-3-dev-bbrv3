# Presentation: BBRv3 in ns-3

`bbrv3-port.pptx`: about one hour, 32 slides, 16:9. Opens in PowerPoint,
Keynote, Google Slides (File > Import) and LibreOffice Impress.

| Part | Slides | Time | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The BBRv3 port | 3-24 | about 35 min | Gavin |
| 2. My research (placeholder) | 25-28 | about 10 min | Gavin |
| 3. Pedro (placeholder) | 29-31 | about 10 min | Pedro |
| Summary and questions | 32 | about 5 min | |

Each slide has speaker notes with the talking points and a time estimate.
The placeholder slides have dashed boxes; replace them with your content.

The figures are screenshots of `../bbrv3-comparisons/visualizations/figures/slides/`
(and `13_stage4_vs_stage6_6_seeds.png`). If the data changes, rerun
`python3 make_figures.py` there and replace the pictures.

Editing: a `.pptx` cannot be merged by git, so pull before editing, edit, and
push soon after, and say in the commit which slides you changed.
